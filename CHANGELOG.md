# Changelog

## [0.3.0] - 2026-11-15

### Added
- Lava Cannon (Danish: Lavabombarde): a plump medieval bombard on a wheeled carriage. Put it down, and use it to fire a ball of lava that leaves lava that stays where it lands. It turns to where you look; sneak and use it to pick the range from 1 to 5 (8, 16, 24, 32 or 40 blocks). A direct hit does 8 damage and burns for 5 s. Two lava cells a shot, one shot every 2 s. Only players can break it, and it drops itself. No lava with `mobGriefing` off.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-14

### Added
- Obsidian Gun: fires lava that sets into obsidian where it lands – the block hit and the four next to it, flat against the face it hit – and the obsidian stays. It also sets lava into obsidian. A mob that is hit takes 6 damage and burns, and the obsidian sets round its feet. Two lava cells a shot. Nothing is placed with `mobGriefing` off.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added
- Lava Pistol, Lava Gun, Magma Sprayer and Magma Grenade, with lava cells and magma charges as ammunition.
- A direct hit from the Lava Gun shuts a mob in a hollow obsidian shell for 5 seconds; then it burns.
- Temporary lava from shots, grenades and the magma golem that disappears after 6 seconds. No lava or obsidian with `mobGriefing` off.
- Magmasteel ingot, sword (sets things on fire), pickaxe (smelts ores as it mines), axe, shovel and armour (full set: fire resistance; boots: lava walker).
- Lava lamps in four colours, a lava tank (12 buckets), magma bricks, polished and chiseled magma and blocks of magmasteel.
- Magma golem and lava slug in the Nether.
- Bedrock add-on and Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3, website in English and Danish.
