# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

### Added
- Lava Pistol, Lava Gun, Magma Sprayer and Magma Grenade, with lava cells and magma charges as ammunition.
- A direct hit from the Lava Gun shuts a mob in a hollow obsidian shell for 5 seconds; then it burns.
- Temporary lava from shots, grenades and the magma golem that disappears after 6 seconds. No lava or obsidian with `mobGriefing` off.
- Magmasteel ingot, sword (sets things on fire), pickaxe (smelts ores as it mines), axe, shovel and armour (full set: fire resistance; boots: lava walker).
- Lava lamps in four colours, a lava tank (12 buckets), magma bricks, polished and chiseled magma and blocks of magmasteel.
- Magma golem and lava slug in the Nether.
- Bedrock add-on and Fabric mod for Minecraft 26.3, website in English and Danish.
